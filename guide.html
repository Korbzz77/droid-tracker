Put it on your home screen

Install

It's a web app, so there's no App Store or Play Store step. Adding it to your home screen gives it an icon, drops the browser bars, and lets it work with no signal.

  1. Open the link above. On iPhone or iPad this has to be Safari — Chrome on iOS can't install it. On Android use Chrome.
  2. Tap the Share button, or the ⋮ menu on Android. Share is the square with an arrow coming out of the top.
  3. Choose "Add to Home Screen", then Add. Done. Open it from the icon from now on.

You can skip all of that and just use it in the browser — everything works, you only lose the offline part.

Do this once

First run

Tell it where you are, and it fills itself in

Top right, set your Cycle, then set your RB number. On a fresh app that ticks off every droid the game asked for on the way there — usually 20 or 30 of them — so you're not entering your whole collection by hand.

Then fix the exceptions: anything you sold, and anything you're further ahead on than the requirements demanded.

The one thing that isn't obvious

The colour dot

Every droid has a small coloured dot next to its name. That dot is a button.

Tap it to say what quality you own

Pick a quality and every requirement at or below it fills in automatically — across all five cycles, because in the game you own one droid at one quality and it counts everywhere.

BASE→GOLD→DIA→ RBOW→BESK→GAL→STEL

So if you own a galactic A-LT, tapping the dot and choosing GAL satisfies the base one at rebirth 3 and the diamond one at rebirth 6 in a single tap.

Tapping the droid's name instead just ticks that one quality, which is handy for a quick correction.

Five tabs in the header

The screens

ROADMAP

The whole cycle

The same chart as in game, all 35 rebirths. Search it to find which rebirths want a particular droid.

BUILD

What to make next

Split into Worker, Astromech and Battle columns, and only ever lists rebirths still ahead of you — so it gets shorter as you go. Each row says whether it's a fresh buy or an upgrade, and the ones you part-own carry an amber stripe.

SELL

What you can let go

Splits what you own into safe to sell and hold on to, based on the cycle you're playing. Tap a droid to sell it; there's an undo if you misfire.

INDEX

Everything you've ever made

All 561 entries — every droid at every quality, plus the eight iconics. Tap one to mark it crafted. Selling never removes anything here, and a diamond doesn't count as a gold. Filter by quality, class or rarity.

FUSION

All 17 recipes

Colour-coded by rarity. Ingredients you own are filled and ticked, missing ones are outlined, and anything you can make right now gets a green Ready flag and jumps to the top of its group.

Top of every screen

Blueprint timers

Three countdowns live in the header, so they're visible wherever you are in the app.

S Stellar · on the hour M Mythic · at :55 G Galactic · at :15 and :45 D Shop deal · on the hour

Tap one to get a warning 20 seconds before it spawns — a banner drops in and it beeps, whatever screen you're on. Tap it again to turn it off. Worth arming Galactic if you're grinding.

Read this bit

Before you rely on it

Your progress lives on that one device

There's no account and nothing is uploaded. That means it won't sync between your phone and your tablet, and deleting the home screen app takes your progress with it.

This matters most for the INDEX. Everything else you can rebuild in a minute by setting your rebirth number; a record of everything you've ever crafted can't be rebuilt at all.

The settings button at the top right has Back up progress — it saves a file you keep. Worth doing after a big session.

Quick answers

Odds and ends

It's showing an old version
Close it fully — swipe it out of the app switcher — and open it again while online. It checks for updates on launch.
Nothing appears on the SELL screen
That's normal early in a cycle: the game still wants everything you own. The screen tells you which rebirth the first one frees up at.
Does it cost anything?
No. No ads, no signup, no permissions.
Something's wrong in the data
Tell Korbz — the requirements were transcribed from the in-game chart and cross-checked, but the game does get updated.
